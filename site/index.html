MinusOne

I play the drums.

    Paste a YouTube link or drop in a song. MinusOne splits it into vocals, drums, bass, guitar and keys, then takes the drums out so you can play along.

    MP3, WAV, M4A or FLAC, up to 10 minutes

    Getting ready

    1. Get the audio
    2. Split into instruments
    3. Load into the mixer

    Your songs

    Songs you split are saved on this device, so they open instantly next time, even offline.

      Splitting runs on your own free server; nothing is sold or shared. Only use music you have the right to practise with.

      MinusOne sends songs to a server that splits them. Use your free Hugging Face Space address, or a computer on your network running the server.
