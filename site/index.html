MinusOne

I play the drums.

    Paste a YouTube link or drop in a song. MinusOne splits it into vocals, drums, bass, guitar and keys, then takes the drums out so you can play along.

    MP3, WAV, M4A or FLAC, up to 10 minutes

    This PC has no NVIDIA graphics card, so splitting here can take 10 to 30 minutes. Add a free cloud key once and songs split on fast cloud GPUs instead, usually in a few minutes.

      Where to split songs

      Sign up free at mvsep.com, then copy the API token from your account (see their API page). Free accounts split one song at a time; at busy times there can be a queue.

      MinusOne server

      Songs are split by the MinusOne server running on your PC. Starting it opens this page already connected; on a phone, scan the QR code it shows. You can also paste the address from the server window here.
